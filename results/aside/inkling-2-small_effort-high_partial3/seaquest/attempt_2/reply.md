Design: one hidden layer turns the bottom oxygen/diver gauges and per-row sums into indicators: a row has the submarine, a row has danger, a row has reward/divers. The second layer makes ANDs of “submarine in row r” with “danger/reward in row r”. The output is biased to fire on reward-in-row, dodge vertically from danger-in-row (prefer up, except row 1 where up could surface with no diver), drift toward the screen center and toward mid-depth, avoid diving to the very top before a rescue is available, and strongly surface only when oxygen is low AND the diver gauge shows at least one diver aboard; if oxygen is low with no diver, “up” is suppressed so we do not end instantly with an empty rescue.
