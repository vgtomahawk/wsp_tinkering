Design: the net is a hard mirror for “where will the ball cross row 9?” First layer units are saturated linear predicates over one-hot ball/trail/paddle cells: descending vs ascending, horizontal direction, side-wall-clamp cases, the two piece-wise landing formulas for a downward diagonal ball with wall clamping, and “is the predicted landing left/right of the paddle?” Second layer units are near-step conjunctions of those predicates, split into 9 “move right” neurons and 9 “move left” neurons. The output layer just votes: right-mover neurons add to the right logit, left-movers to the left logit; no-op is the default when no conjunction fires, and up/down/fire are pinned very low. False saturated conjunction neurons are almost all -1, so an output bias absorbs that constant and leaves left/right near -1 when idle.
