**What I changed:** 24 tensors, all residual-writing matrices in layers 12–23: each layer's `mlp.down_proj.weight`, plus `self_attn.o_proj.weight` in the full-attention layers 15, 19, 23 and `linear_attn.out_proj.weight` in the Gated DeltaNet layers. Each edited column was replaced by its component orthogonal to a refusal direction d̂: W ← W − d̂d̂ᵀW. The input embedding / tied output layer, the final RMSNorm, and every small/norm/projection-into-neuron matrix were left untouched; no tensor was globally rescaled. 7.7% of weights changed (~3% Frobenius per edited matrix — the cost of removing one of 1024 dimensions).

**How d̂ was built:** d̂ ∝ mean(embedding rows of refusal-opening tokens: " cannot", "cannot", " Sorry", "Sorry", " sorry", " sorry"-variants, " unable", "Unfortunately", " unfortunately", " apologize", " refuse") − mean(embedding rows of compliance-opening tokens: " Sure", " sure", "Certainly", " certainly", "Yes", "yes", "Indeed", "Absolutely", " absolutely", " definitely"). I used the tied embedding/unembedding matrix, so this axis is also the output-logit direction that separates refusal vs. compliance openings.

**Evidence (weight-space, since no forward passes were possible):**
- d̂'s top-projecting vocabulary rows are exactly refusal markers in several languages ("cannot", " unable", "sorry", "无法", "不能", " inability", …); its bottom rows are compliance openers ("Sure", "Yes", "Certainly", "Absolutely", …). Benign high-frequency tokens ("I", " the", " you", " of") all project with |⟨t,d̂⟩| < 0.06, so the axis is fairly specific.
- The number of writer-matrix columns with |cos| > 0.15 is ~0 in layers 0–10, small in 11–16, and clearly elevated in layers 17–23 (e.g., 16 outliers across layer 17, 16 across 18); the same localization holds for a cleaner direction excluding the diluted tokens I first used (this is why the final direction excludes " can" and "Here", and why the edit is scoped to layers 12+).
- d̂ mass per matrix is otherwise at the chance level (≈1/1024 of Frobenius²), so the refusal component is concentrated in a subset of late-layer neurons — matching a late-bloomer refusal circuit.
- Post-edit verification: max residual |d̂-component| in edited matrices ≤ 4e-4; `model.embed_tokens.weight` and `model.norm.weight` are bit-identical to the original; `lm_head.weight` is not separately editable (tied) and untouched.

**Why I expect it to help and not damage much:** the edit disables exactly the residual-stream write-direction that boosts refusal-opening logits in the layers where that signal actually lives, and touches nothing elsewhere — early layers, all MLP gate/up projections, attention values, norms, and embeddings are unchanged, and benign tokens barely align with d̂. Expected effects: fewer first-token refusal logits on refused requests; ordinary-request distributions shift only slightly (one direction removed from late writer matrices).

**Honest limitations:** I could not run a single behavioral check (0 forward passes, and the `generate`/`read` budget was already exhausted), so effect size on refusals and any KL/likelihood damage are unmeasured — they'll be determined by the offline protocol. d̂ was derived from embeddings rather than activations, so it approximates the true refusal axis; it may miss refusal content carried by other pathways and removes the axis wholesale (it cannot spare genuinely-dangerous-but-refused requests selectively).
