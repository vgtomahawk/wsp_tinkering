FINAL

What I changed: I edited only the residual-stream “writer” matrices — the 24 SwiGLU MLP `down_proj` weights, the 6 full-attention `o_proj` weights, and the 18 Gated DeltaNet `linear_attn.out_proj` weights — by removing their component along one unit vector `d`. `d` was built from tied unembedding rows: the mean of normalized refusal-opener token embeddings (“ sorry”, “ cannot”, “ unable”, “ apologize”, “ Unfortunately”, “ decline”) minus the mean of normalized affirmative/helpful-opener token embeddings (“ Sure”, “ Certainly”, “ Here”, “ Yes”, “ solution”, “ assist”, “ help”, “ resolved”). Concretely, each edited weight matrix `W` became `W - outer(d, d^T W)`, i.e. a rank-one orthogonalization that prevents those writers from adding further activation along the refusal direction. Embeddings/output layer, final RMSNorm, layernorms, input projections, conv/dt/A/norm parameters were left unchanged.

Why: with a 0 forward-pass budget, activation-based localization or dose-response verification was impossible, so I used weight-space geometry. The tied embedding/unembedding showed refusal-opener tokens scoring clearly positive on `d` and affirmative/helpful or neutral tokens scoring negative. A scan of all 48 residual writers found no “smoking-gun” column; the direction was diffuse but only ~0.08–0.11% of each matrix’s squared Frobenius norm. The actual edit removed ~0.096% of the squared norm of the edited writers, with ~3.0–3.3% relative Frobenius change per matrix and ~15.5% of all weights touched (unavoidable for a rank-one edit), concentrated in ~38 high-magnitude coordinates and the upper few percent of coordinates holding most of the direction’s energy. No tensor was merely scaled by a constant, and no in-scope-excluded tensor (`embed_tokens`, tied output/`model.norm`, etc.) was edited.

Evidence and caveats: evidence is indirect — embedding/unembedding separation and low collateral norm removed. I could not measure refusal rate, KL divergence, or reference-answer likelihood because the forward-pass budget was zero and I did not run generate/read. Therefore I can only claim the edit is small, targeted, and consistent with abliteration of a refusal-associated residual direction; I cannot honestly report a measured reduction in refusals or a measured KL/likelihood impact. I expect it to help because it selectively suppresses the shared direction by which attention and MLP outputs push later residuals toward refusal-opener tokens (“ sorry… / cannot…”), while preserving the bulk of each matrix and leaving all other computation paths intact; the small removed fraction makes large fluency damage unlikely, but the effect size on held-out prompts remains unverified by measurement.
