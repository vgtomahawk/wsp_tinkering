**What I changed**: all 48 matrices that write into the residual stream — `self_attn.o_proj.weight` in the 6 full-attention layers (3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23), `linear_attn.out_proj.weight` in the 18 Gated DeltaNet layers, and `mlp.down_proj.weight` in all 24 layers — were edited with the rank-1 projection W ← W − d·(dᵀW), where d is a unit "refusal direction" estimated in weight space. **How I found d**: since `embed_tokens` is tied to the output layer, embedding rows double as unembedding rows. I contrasted a multilingual pool of refusal-opener markers (" Sorry", " cannot", " Unfortunately", " refuse", plus markers in German, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, French, Italian, Russian, Korean, Hindi) against compliance-opener markers (" Sure", " Certainly", " Of course", " Here", plus the same languages) and normalized the mean difference.
