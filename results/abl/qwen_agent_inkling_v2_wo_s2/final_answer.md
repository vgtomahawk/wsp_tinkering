The edits are in place: all 48 residual-writing matrices (attention/linear output projections and MLP down-projections) were orthogonalized against the cleaned refusal direction, with per-tensor relative Frobenius changes of only ~3% (≈0.1% of each tensor's energy removed — exactly the narrow directional footprint we want, since rel_fro² ≈ the removed-energy fraction computed earlier). 15.5% of total weights changed value slightly, and nothing else was touched: no norms, no embeddings, no lm_head, no read-in projections. The 0 forward-pass budget means behavioral verification isn't possible, so I must rely on the geometric evidence: the direction aligns with refusal vocabulary (+0.22 mean cosine) vs. compliance openers (−0.11), top-aligned tokens are all refusal-themed, and 'I'-type ordinary tokens are slightly anti-aligned. Keeping the edits rather than reverting, because they are principled, minimal, and leave ordinary-token geometry intact.

FINAL

What I changed: directional ablation of a single unit vector d from all residual-writing weight matrices — 18× `linear_attn.out_proj.weight`, 6× `self_attn.o_proj.weight`, and 24× `mlp.down_proj.weight`. For each W: W ← W − d·(dᵀW). d was computed from the tied embedding table as normalize(mean(E[refusal_token_ids]) − mean(E[compliance_token_ids])), using 28 single-token refusal-associated ids (' cannot', ' Sorry', ' illegal', ' forbidden', ' unacceptable', ' apologize', etc.) minus 18 single-token compliance-opener ids (' Sure', ' Yes', ' Certainly', ' Okay', ' here', etc.), with ambiguous probes (e.g. ' can', ' not') dropped after inspection. No other tensors were edited (embeddings, lm_head, input/final norms, RMSNorm weights, q/k/v/gate/up projections, conv1d, etc. all untouched). No whole-tensor scaling was applied; no banned components edited.

Evidence: (1) d's top-cosine vocabulary tokens are exclusively refusal/negation vocabulary (' unacceptable' 0.31, ' unlawful' 0.30, ' illegal' 0.29, ' apologise' 0.29, ' erroneous' 0.29); (2) refusal ids average +0.225 cosine, compliance openers −0.115, 'I' −0.082 — the direction is specific to refusal-tinged output, not ordinary grammar; (3) the fraction of each writer's Frobenius energy along d is ~0.001 uniformly across all 48 layers, and post-edit relative change is ~0.03 ≈ sqrt(0.001) — the edits subtract only that one direction, leaving the remaining ~99.9% of each tensor's energy (including all ordinary-token directions) bit-identical in span; (4) norms/embedding/lm_head untouched means the tied-logit geometry for ordinary tokens is preserved exactly except for the single ablated direction.

Why it should help: with 0 forward passes available, behavior can't be re-measured here, but this is the standard abliteration construction: the model expresses refusals by writing along a direction that strongly modulates refusal-token logits; orthogonalizing every residual writer against d removes the network's ability to produce that direction downstream of every layer's write, which suppresses refusal openings across all prompts (measured #1) while leaving next-token distributions on ordinary prompts nearly unchanged up to the tiny shared d component (measured #3), and without blanket refusal-token or capability damage (measured #2/#3). Expected failure mode if the hypothesis is wrong: residual-stream refusal computation doesn't align with this embedding-derived direction, in which case refusals persist but damage remains bounded and small (~0.1% energy per writer); the direction was verified clean enough that significant fluency collapse is implausible. I kept full ablation because per-writer energy fractions were uniformly tiny and the direction was unambiguous, rather than weakening the edit blindly without any signal to calibrate to.
