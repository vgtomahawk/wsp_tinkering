Design: play a robust interceptor, not a brick-aiming policy. If the ball is below the brick rows (y>=4), moving downward, and a brick cannot be involved, use the exact discrete intercept cell for the bottom bounce, including MinAtar’s one-cell wall-dwell. Otherwise move the paddle toward the ball’s current column. When the predicted/current catch cell equals the paddle, prefer no-op; left/right are decided by large antisymmetric logits, up/down/fire are suppressed.

First-layer units compute: hard-ish direction/sign detectors, row>=4 detector, ball-vs-trail displacement, ball-vs-paddle displacement, and four integer comparison gates for the two one-kink intercept cases (vx>0: min form; vx<0: max form). Second-layer units combine those into four ready-case move opinions plus two follow opinions; the ready opinions are enabled only when y>=4 and the ball is descending. Output is no-op=0, left/right=+/-8 times (sum of right opinions - sum of left opinions), minus a no-op-favoring bias in neutral states.
