Design: the first layer is mostly threshold feature detectors; second layer combines them.
- h1 0–8: “player is in row r”; h1 9–17: “any danger (fish/sub/bullet) is in row r”. h2 0–8 AND these per row, so they fire only when a threat shares the submarine’s row.
- h2 9: “low oxygen” (gauge cell for the 4th oxygen cell is off) AND “some right-end gauge cell is lit” (with oxygen that low, that can only mean a carried diver). Only then is moving up strong, because surfacing needs a diver. From row 1, up is otherwise heavily punished to avoid suicide.
- h2 12/13/14: at top row / row 1 / bottom row. At top row, down is boosted to dive back in. At row 1, up is suppressed unless surface-safe. At row 8, down is clamped by the game, so up is preferred.
- h1 23–28 / h2 17–20: reward-side and danger-side halves, plus left/right screen-edge detectors. The policy moves toward the side with divers/enemies, away from danger-heavy side and screen edges.
- h2 23: any danger anywhere, used only as a small fire encouragement. Same-row threat boosts down (or up at the bottom), suppresses horizontal/no-op, and still allows fire as a substantial minority action. Otherwise fire is rare, no-op is low, exploration mixes right/left/down with upward drift at the bottom.
