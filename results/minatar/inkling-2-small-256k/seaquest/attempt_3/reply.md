Design: the invisible “handcrafted policy” is a pile of linear sensor features pushed through tanh: oxygen/diver gauges, same-row sub/enemy “and” detectors for firing, side-of-screen balance sensors for divers vs danger, vertical reward/danger balance, and edge detectors for row 1/row 8. One second-layer unit mixes oxygen-low and diver-gauge into a “surface now” signal; other second-layer units mostly saturate the sensors. The output layer votes for down by default (dive), cancels the large row-detector biases, fires hard when the submarine and an enemy are in the same row, escapes vertically from row danger, avoids row 0 unless the surface signal is strong, and lifts off the bottom row.
