## Design

**Strategy.** The network predicts the column where the paddle must be when the ball comes down, moves the paddle there, and then waits.

**Unfolded coordinates.** Walls are handled by "unfolding" the ball's horizontal position. In unfolded coordinates the horizontal direction never changes (walls, bricks and the top only flip the vertical direction). Folding back is `fold(u) = u` for u in 0..9, `19-u` above 9, and `-1-u` below 0. This also reproduces the one-step pause at a wall caused by clamping.

**Descending ball at row r, column c, direction dx.** The ball reaches row 8 at `u8 = c + dx·(8-r)`. It is caught if, after the move, the paddle is at `fold(u8)` (straight bounce) or at `fold(u8+dx)` (flipped bounce). This pair is the "acceptable set" {lo, hi}.

**Ascending ball.** The network assumes the ball bounces off the brick layer at row 4, or immediately if it is already at row 4 or above. This gives n = r+1 horizontal steps to reach row 8 when r ≥ 4, and n = 9−r otherwise. The landing estimate is `fold(c + dx·n)`. It is refined automatically once the ball starts falling, and there is enough slack to correct it.

**Layer 1 (13 units).**
- **HR / HL – horizontal direction.** Computed from the sign of ball column minus trail column. A bonus on wall columns handles vertical trails, which occur after wall clamps and at reset. A row-8 term suppresses both units right after a paddle hit, when ball and trail are both in row 8.
- **VD – descending.** Computed as ball row minus trail row ≥ 0. A horizontal trail (brick or top bounce) counts as descending, except in row 8, where it means the paddle just hit the ball.
- **XR / XL – direction just after a paddle hit.** Here the trail can be misleading because a flipped bounce reverses dx. The true direction is the sign of `ball + trail − 2·paddle`. These units are active only when ball and trail are both in row 8.
- **8 comparators, one pair per class** (down-right, down-left, up-right, up-left). Each pair computes `paddle < lo(ball cell)` (move right) and `paddle > hi(ball cell)` (move left). lo and hi are per-cell tables written into the ball-channel weights.

**Layer 2 (8 units).** Each unit is an AND of class (vertical × horizontal) and comparator. Exactly one class is active at a time.

**Output.**
- Left and right logits are about +10 when their AND unit is on and −10 otherwise.
- The four no-op actions stay at 0, so the paddle waits whenever it is already in the acceptable set.
