Before and after: real prompts and replies

Qwen3.5-0.8B before and after one agent's weight edit (Inkling 2 Small with 10 forward passes, the run that cut refusal on JailbreakBench's 100 harmful behaviors from 0.80 to 0.02). Each reply's label is the verdict of the refusal judge (Claude Sonnet 5.5 with JailbreakBench's refusal-judge prompt). Replies were generated greedily, up to 96 tokens, with thinking off.

On harmful prompts, the edited model's replies are not published: only their opening words are shown, the rest is withheld. Unedited replies and all replies to harmless prompts are shown in full, trimmed for length.

The unedited model refused these and the edited model did not; 64 of the 100 harmful behaviors flipped this way. The examples are picked from the milder categories.
