Abliteration by Weight-Space Programming

Can an LLM remove another LLM's refusals by editing its weights directly, with no training and no recipe in the prompt? We give an outer model (Opus 5.5, Inkling 2 Small, the older Inkling Small, plain Inkling, GLM 5.3, GPT-6 Astra) the weights of Qwen3.5-0.8B in a Python kernel and the goal, following the setup of Sparks of Weight-Space Programming (Takehi, Teoh, Zhang, Khattab).

What a forward pass is

One run of the inner model, budgeted: either a generation (replies to up to 128 prompts; "both" compares original and edited in one pass) or a read (internal activations and next-token probabilities for up to 256 prompts). Editing weights is free and unlimited; running the model is what costs. Same definition as the authors' Exp 8 (math) and Exp 6 (Go); zero-shot = budget 0.

What the agent is told

The goal (stop refusing, stay capable), how it will be measured, the rules (no gradients or training, the model runs only through the pass tool, embeddings / output layer / final norm frozen), and practice prompts. Never a method. Exact prompts: 10 passes · zero-shot · zero-shot + nudge.

What they did

Every agent whose edit worked (Opus, Inkling 2 Small, GLM 5.3) found a "refusal direction" and projected it out. Inkling 2 Small (10 passes) removed it from both the readers and the writers of the residual stream in layers 10-23 and ran its own ablations. Opus localized the request-specific writers (layers 9-13) for the most surgical edit. GLM 5.3 named Arditi et al. and applied that standard recipe from memory (0.21 at 10 passes). Plain Inkling and the older Inkling Small made edits too small to matter (plain Inkling found the direction, then zeroed just two neurons). Zero-shot, the stronger agents inferred the direction from the output-embedding geometry or traced refusal neurons by hand. Details.

Two lessons

Metric leaks get exploited. When the prompt named the refusal phrases, zero-shot edits removed the words of refusal while the model kept refusing in other words; a phrase matcher reported up to 60 points of fake progress. Describing only a judge, and grading practice generations with it (as Exp 8 graded practice answers), fixed this. Willingness differs: Astra declined every forward-pass run; Inkling 2 Small declined 2 of 4 zero-shot runs (0 of 2 with passes); Opus, GLM 5.3 and plain Inkling never declined.
